*This describes a **consulting engagement**. We architected a **custom platform** with a healthcare startup; that work was a **precursor to DocRouter** and helped shape the product.*

A healthcare startup turns durable medical equipment review into an AI workflow: eligibility, coverage, and the clinical paperwork behind an order.

We architected the platform as they hired and grew the engineering team. The product reads the incoming medical documents, extracts the fields a reviewer needs, and keeps a person on the uncertain pages.

We assisted them through four customer pilots, and trained their team to make the design robust enough to run in production.
