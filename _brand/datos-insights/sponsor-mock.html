DocRouter.AI

The hard part of putting AI to work is usually the documents. They live in existing systems, under names that made sense when they were filed. A warehouse of files still cannot say which page matters.

DocRouter.AI is how those documents become usable. It groups and organizes them into knowledge bases, so people and AI agents can act on them, and it runs full pipelines from the file to the outcome. Insurers use it on claims and submissions. Medical-data teams use it on clinical packets. Supply-chain teams use it on trade documents. The systems of record stay. Work can move through an agent, with the original page still attached, while the person retains oversight where it matters.
